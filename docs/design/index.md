# Design notes

Working notes on why things are shaped the way they are: what was tried, what
was measured, what the hardware turned out to do. They are dated where it
matters and are not maintained as manuals. The user-facing and developer-facing
pages link here when a decision needs its history.

Nothing has been moved into this section yet. The candidates, currently
embedded in other pages, are the CarPlay port history in
[carplay](../nodes/carplay.html), the renderer and performance notes in
[map_server](../nodes/map_server.html), the reverse-engineered protocol in
[can_motec](../libs/can_motec.html), the per-panel rationale in
[scope](../apps/scope.html), and the derivation notes in
[tracks](../tools/tracks.html).
