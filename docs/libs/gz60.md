# gz60

## Overview

The on-chip modules of the Freescale MC68HC908GZ60 around an [hc08](hc08.html)
core: the system integration module, the clock generator, the ports, the COP
watchdog, the two timers and the timebase, the ADC, flash, and the MSCAN08 CAN
controller, composed as `Gz60`. A board model connects its pins to the world;
[pdm32_sim](../sims/pdm32.html) is the one in the tree.

Each module models what firmware observes: register reads and writes, interrupt
timing, and flash programming and erasing. Where firmware touches something the
model does not implement, the module logs a `model gap:` warning once rather
than failing.

## Behaviour worth knowing

MSCAN times its transmissions from the bit rate the firmware programmed, so a
frame takes as long as it would on the wire. It refuses CAN FD frames, as a
classic controller sees them as form errors, and transmits a DLC of 9 to 15 as
eight bytes.
